## 🧠 Discernment & Rejection Log (The "Kill Your Darlings" Note)

### Rejected Image 1: 3D Glowing Cybernetic Brain / Neon Network Nodes
- **Why Generated**: Prompted AI for "cool AI brain for machine learning hero background".
- **Why Rejected**: The generated image had neon glowing 3D brains and sci-fi laser grids. While visually flashy, it looked like generic stock art and directly violated the Voice Card rule (*"No buzzwords, plain technical focus"*). It upstaged the work and reduced technical credibility.
- **Replacement Choice**: Replaced with a clean, 2D architectural diagram (`workflow_diagram.png`) on dark navy canvas.

### Rejected Image 2: AI-Generated Human Software Engineer Avatar
- **Why Generated**: Prompted AI for "photo of a young male software engineer in office".
- **Why Rejected**: The AI-generated face had subtle rendering artifacts (unnatural lighting and smooth skin texture) that made it look fake. Using a fake AI avatar destroys recruiter trust.
- **Replacement Choice**: Used Ghulam Qasim's real photograph from the Anthropic Academy enrollment verification (`03_Anthropic_Academy_Enrollment.png.png`).
